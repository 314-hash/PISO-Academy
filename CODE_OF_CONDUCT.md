# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in PISO Academy a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community—embodying the Filipino spirit of **Bayanihan** (communal unity and cooperation).

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leadership at `conduct@piso-chain.org`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
